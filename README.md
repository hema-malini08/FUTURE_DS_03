# Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing customer movement across the marketing funnel to understand how users progress from website traffic to final customer conversion.

The analysis evaluates conversion performance, funnel drop-offs, and cart abandonment behaviour to identify key business challenges affecting customer conversions and revenue growth.

---

## Tools Used
- Python (Data Cleaning & Funnel Analysis)
- Power BI (Dashboard & Data Visualization)
- DAX (KPI Calculations)

---

## Dataset
- Source: Generated Marketing Funnel Dataset from Kaggle

---

## Business Questions
- Where are users dropping off in the funnel?
- Which funnel stages have the highest customer loss?
- How can conversion rates be improved?
- What factors contribute to cart abandonment?
- Which stages require optimization to improve overall conversions?

---

## Key Metrics
- Total Traffic: 100,000
- Traffic to Lead Rate: 96.08%
- Lead to Cart Rate: 96.22%
- Cart to Customer Rate: 49.73%
- Overall Conversion Rate: 45.98%
- Cart Abandonment Rate: 50.27%

---

## Key Insights
- The highest drop-off occurs during the Cart Lead → Customer stage with a drop-off rate of 50.27%.
- The funnel performs strongly during the Traffic → Lead and Lead → Cart stages with conversion rates above 96%.
- Users actively add products to the cart, indicating strong purchase intent and engagement.
- The high cart abandonment rate suggests friction during checkout or payment completion.
- The checkout stage is the primary area requiring optimization to improve overall conversions.

---

## Business Recommendations
- Simplify the checkout process to improve purchase completion rates.
- Implement cart reminder emails and promotional offers to reduce cart abandonment.
- Provide multiple secure payment options for better customer convenience.
- Improve customer trust through reviews, secure payment badges, and transparent policies.
- Continuously monitor funnel performance to identify conversion improvement opportunities.

---

## Business Impact
By implementing these strategies, the business can achieve:
- Improved customer conversion rates.
- Reduced cart abandonment and revenue loss.
- Better customer experience and engagement.
- Increased marketing ROI and business profitability.
- Stronger overall funnel performance and revenue growth.

---

## Conclusion
The analysis identified strong performance during lead generation and cart engagement stages. However, significant customer loss occurs during the final purchase stage due to high cart abandonment.

By optimizing checkout experience and reducing funnel drop-offs, the business can improve conversion efficiency, customer acquisition, and overall revenue growth.
